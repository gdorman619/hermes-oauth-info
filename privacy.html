Privacy policy

This policy describes a personal-use assistant. These public pages are separate from the private assistant.

How Google data is used

With authorization, Hermes reads Gmail messages to find and summarize information; its Gmail permission is read-only. It reads and manages Calendar events, Drive files (including sharing or deletion), Sheets spreadsheets, and Tasks lists and items to carry out requested or scheduled personal-assistant tasks. These services may allow records to be created, changed, or removed at the owner's direction. Google data is not sold, used for advertising, or published on this website.

Storage and processing

Google authorization credentials and operational records are stored in the owner's private assistant environment. Retrieved content and summaries may appear in conversation histories, logs, or task outputs. Relevant data may be processed by an AI provider to fulfill a request, and requested results may be delivered through the owner's connected messaging services. This public website does not host Google account data or credentials.

Retention and control

Operational records may remain until the owner removes them or changes retention settings. The owner can revoke Google access in Google Account settings and remove locally stored credentials. Revocation stops new Google API access but does not automatically remove previously created records or delivered messages.

Public website

This informational website has no account registration, contact form, advertising, or owner-installed analytics. Its hosting provider may process ordinary web request data such as IP address and browser information to deliver and protect the pages. No Google account data is published here.

Changes

If how Google data is used changes, this policy must be updated before the new use. The Google authorization screen provides the app's support contact.
